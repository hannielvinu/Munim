# Munim agent card

SIMULATED catalogue, offline buyers, MOCK Rezolve.

Can negotiate price within policy, quantity and bundle tiers. Cannot negotiate below floor, unavailable stock, hidden fees or facts outside the catalogue. Pressure is recorded and excluded from pricing. The ladder rewards changed commitments; the selected baseline is explicitly time-based. Seller speech never includes private cost/floor/buffer. This is a local demonstration, not a payment or inventory reservation system.
